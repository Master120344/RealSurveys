Privacy, in plain language.

This page describes the current preview, not a future paid service.

Trying a sample survey

Guest mode does not send your answers, final message, or optional information to us or to a company. Those entries exist only on the current page and are discarded when you finish or leave. Your guest status and completed survey IDs are stored in this browser tab’s session storage, so we can show your demo progress.

Preview credits are local display values. They are not money, and completion markers do not prove a person’s identity or enforce one response per person.

Anonymous is the default

The final feedback step defaults to anonymous. If you choose to add information, every field is optional. “Never mind” clears the details and returns you to anonymous. Nothing in that form is sent during the preview.

Existing accounts

Email sign-in, account creation, password reset, and account balance retrieval use the existing Firebase service. Registering sends your email and password to Firebase Authentication and creates an account record. Guest browsing doesn’t load Firebase.

No advertising or cross-site tracking

This refreshed preview includes no advertising, analytics pixels, or cross-site tracking scripts. Its fonts and company artwork are hosted with the site. The hosting and account providers may process technical connection information to operate their services.

Before paid surveys launch

We’ll need to explain exactly who receives each survey’s responses, how long those responses are kept, how to request deletion, how identity checks work, and how payments work. The intended model is to provide feedback only to the company identified in the survey, with optional contact details shared only by choice.
